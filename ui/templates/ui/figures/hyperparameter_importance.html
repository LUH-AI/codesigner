{% comment %}Tunability is what tuning a hyperparameter *could* gain, measured from the config space's default. That is the right question before a search and the wrong one during it: by the time it says a hyperparameter is worth 98%, the optimizer has usually already taken most of that 98%, and the figure is spending itself on a settled question. Ticking this asks the other one — what is left — by subtracting what the selected trial has already banked. A request rather than a second precomputed view: what has been banked depends on which trial is selected, so it is fetched, and the page costs nothing extra while the box is clear.{% endcomment %} {% comment %}Wrapped so the page can take the whole control away at once: both halves of "still to gain" are tunability's, so it is hidden under the other two games rather than left there offering an answer it cannot give.
