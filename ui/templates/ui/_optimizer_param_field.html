A decimal is a plain text field. `type="number"` puts arrows on it that step by one, which on a fraction between zero and one is the whole range — useless in themselves, and they make the field look like it wants a whole number. Stepping is left to the settings where a step of one is a real increment. The accepted range is part of what the circled i says (see `info_for`), and what an empty field would use is the field's own placeholder.
