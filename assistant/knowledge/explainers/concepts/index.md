# Concept explainers (draft)

- `metrics.md` (concept-metrics): every metric, its direction and range, the know-nothing score a failed trial gets, and the metric's scale.
- `evaluation.md` (concept-evaluation): cross-validation, held-out share, backtests; time order, gap, horizon, series, season; forecasting by reduction and its baselines.
- `tasks-and-models.md` (concept-tasks-and-models): classification vs regression (and forecasting as a way of evaluating), the target column, the built-in models, custom model uploads.
- `optimizers.md` (concept-optimizers): SMAC and every setting, Random Search, Grid Search, and what only SMAC can do.
- `initial-design.md` (concept-initial-design): the model-free first trials, their size, why priors cannot reach them, and delay decay.
- `stopping.md` (concept-stopping): "Run until…" limits, "Give up if…" failure limits, and model-fit deadlines.
- `explanation-games.md` (concept-explanation-games): HyperSHAP tunability, mistunability and sensitivity, interactions, the "Compare against" baseline, local explanation and local effects.
- `surrogate-and-acquisition.md` (concept-surrogate-and-acquisition): the surrogate and its uncertainty, EI/PI/LCB, the prediction sample, Next Trials and proposals.
- `priors.md` (concept-priors): prior kinds, decay and β / N, how SMAC uses them, and Evaluate Prior.
- `projection.md` (concept-projection): HP Space, PCA and PLS views, and their layers.
- `deferred-computation.md` (concept-deferred-computation): what is computed at the end of a run, on every page load, or on request, and what happens during a run.
- `permissions.md` (concept-permissions): owners, groups, roles, sharing, and what the assistant may never do.
